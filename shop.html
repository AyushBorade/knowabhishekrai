HUMBLE.

“Vantage points gave me an even greater sense of height.”

HUMBLE. IT IS A COLLECTION OF VISUALS FROM A YEAR, SHARING STORIES BEHIND EACH SHOT AND MY EVERY THOUGHT, CULMINATING IN THE SECONDS BEFORE SNAPPING IT.

VOL 01 INCLUDES 12 PHOTOS.

ABOUT AUTHOR

“I am a creative professional working across multiple streams, shaping visual stories through commercial film production and post-production, along with many forms of media.

My interest in this photo issue comes from a deep urge to explore how a single frame can hold an entire inner world.

I’m driven by what I call that moment when I zone out, connect to an idea, and catch a striking visual born from natural timing and atmosphere.

This project is my attempt to honour those fleeting instances that feel almost imagined yet completely real when captured.

Ultimately, I want to document moments that slip past most eyes and see whether the art I create resonates with someone else’s quiet, personal truth.”

- Abhishek Rai

DISAPPEARANCE IS A PATH.

01

The railing guides your eye straight into the haze. where everything slowly fades out. It's like the view is reminding you that sometimes the things we lose still show us where to go.

GRATITUDE FINDS YOU.

02

In all the noise, the A Hard Day's Night cover on the wall feels like a quiet reminder of coming home to yourself. Sometimes gratitude shows up just like that after a long day, in a small moment, when you finally sit down and think about all the lyrics that didn't make sense back then.

SOUND THAT SPEAKS.

03

With LIFE on one side, Dylan saying don't look back on the other, and The Beatles floating above a crack in the wall, the frame feels like music stitching time together. It's a reminder that sound becomes a bridge, carrying us from who we were to who we're becoming.
